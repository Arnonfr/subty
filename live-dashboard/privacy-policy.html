Subty Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Subty is an Android app for finding, downloading, and translating subtitles. It does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or media library.

Anonymous usage events

Subty uses Firebase Firestore for anonymous operational events: searches submitted, subtitle downloads, translation starts and completions, selected languages, and coarse counts. Events use a random app-install identifier, not an account or personal profile. You can turn off anonymous usage events in Settings.

Subtitle text, translated text, downloaded files, API keys, and device folders are never sent as usage events. Search words are sent only when you explicitly enable Include search words in Settings.

Other processing

Search queries and selected title details are sent directly to the title and subtitle providers needed to perform your request. Translation runs on-device after optional Google ML Kit language-model downloads. Search history, settings, and downloaded files are stored locally on your device.
